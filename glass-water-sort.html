Glass Water Sort: Color Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Last updated 2 October 2026

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up. Glass Water Sort never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, stars, coins, collection and settings are stored on your device. The game has no server of its own and we cannot see them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. You can limit or reset it at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and the game contains no analytics or tracking tools of our own.

1. Who we are

Glass Water Sort is a color sorting puzzle game for Android, published on Google Play by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies to the Glass Water Sort app and to this page. A link to this page is in the game under Settings → Privacy policy.

Questions or requests: fundeveloper27@gmail.com

2. Information kept only on your device

To remember your game, Glass Water Sort saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us.

WhatWhy
Your level, stars, coins and helpers (hints, undos, extra bottles)So you continue where you left off.
Your Glass Collection, achievements, event tokens, daily streak, piggy bank and play countsTo show your collection, goals and stats, and to pay the prizes you earn.
Your chosen scene, bottle and liquidSo the game looks the way you set it.
Settings: music, sound, vibration, daily reminder, colour-blind marksTo keep your choices between sessions.
When the last full-screen ad was shownTo space full-screen ads out.

Your consent choice for ads (see section 3.1) is stored on your device by Google's consent tool.

3. Information that leaves your device

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob

Glass Water Sort shows ads served by Google AdMob: full-screen ads between some levels, and videos you can choose to watch for a reward. To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be inferred;
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about ad loading.

Where the law requires it — including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. If you do not agree, ads can still appear but are not personalised. Wherever that message applies, you can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance. Google may also use information from the SDK as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your controls: on Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads on some phones) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Watching a video is always optional.

3.2 Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates. The game may show Google Play's own rating card; your rating goes to Google Play, not to us.

4. What Glass Water Sort does not do

  • No accounts, and no collection of your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No location permission, no camera, no microphone, no access to your files or photos.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDK of our own, and no server that stores your data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No selling of personal information.

6. How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your device: kept until you remove it. Delete everything at once with Android Settings → Apps → Glass Water Sort → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the game.
  • With us: nothing — we do not hold a copy.
  • With Google: handled under Google's own policies (section 3.1).

7. Children

Glass Water Sort is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Ads are reached over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because your progress lives on your phone, the best protection for it is your device's own screen lock and updates.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR in Europe, UK data protection law, or US state laws such as California's — you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to opt out of targeted advertising. Because the game keeps your information on your device, you can exercise most of these directly as described in section 6. For information Google processes for ads, use Google's controls linked in section 3.1. For anything else, email us and we will answer within 30 days.
