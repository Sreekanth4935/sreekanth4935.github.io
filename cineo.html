Cineo Privacy Policy

Cineo: Video Player All Format · Effective 8 October 2026

Cineo is a video player for Android. It plays the videos stored on your phone and the links you choose to open. This page explains what information is involved and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

To show and play your videos, Cineo reads the videos on your phone that you allow it to see. The following are kept only on your phone:

  • your video library, folders and thumbnails;
  • watch history, resume points, favourites and playlists;
  • settings, the equalizer and sound-delay choices;
  • the private folder and its PIN (stored as a one-way hash);
  • stream and playlist addresses you type or open.

Cineo has no account and no server. We do not receive, sell or share any of this information. Uninstalling Cineo removes it from your phone.

Network features

When you open a link, a playlist file or a media server on your home Wi-Fi, Cineo connects directly to that address to play it. Those connections go between your phone and the address you chose; they do not pass through us.

Advertising

The free version of Cineo shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address;
  • information about how ads are seen and tapped, and diagnostic data.

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Where the law requires it (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Cineo asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; Cineo only learns whether the purchase was made, so that it can turn ads off and restore them on a new phone.

Permissions

  • Videos — to show and play the videos on your phone. You can allow all videos or only the ones you pick.
  • Internet and Wi-Fi — to play links and media servers you open, and to show ads.
  • Background playback — only when you turn on background play, so a video can keep playing with its controls in the notification shade.

Children

Cineo is not directed at children under the age of 16 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
