Privacy Policy for QR Vault

QR Vault is a simple QR code scanner and saver app. We respect your privacy.

Information Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data from users.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used only to scan QR codes.
  • Storage: Used to save scanned QR codes locally on your device.

Data Usage

All scanned data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or share your data.
